If you've used Messenger, you already know how this works. [b]HF Convo Anywhere[/b] turns Convo into a chat dock that follows you to every page of Hack Forums. Your DMs, group chats and the global chat are always one click away, so you can keep browsing threads and still reply in seconds.

No setup: install it, refresh HF, and your chats are there. Want it your way? Pick bubbles or a list, left or right side, one of 15 color themes, sounds and more, all from one settings page.

[size=x-large]Features[/size]
[list]
[*][size=large]Chat From Any Page[/size]
Avatar bubbles down the side of every page, with online status, unread badges and typing indicators.
[spoiler=Bubbles and Global Chat][img]https://github.com/xadamxk/HF-Convo-Anywhere/blob/main/store/1-bubbles-and-global-chat.png?raw=true[/img][/spoiler]
[*][size=large]Messenger-Style List[/size]
Prefer a list? See every conversation with its latest message, filtered by All, Direct or Groups.
[spoiler=List Layout][img]https://github.com/xadamxk/HF-Convo-Anywhere/blob/main/store/4-list-layout.png?raw=true[/img][/spoiler]
[*][size=large]Commands and Mentions[/size]
Type / for every Convo command (/flip, /jackpot, /stats, /price and more), or @ to pick a member to mention.
[spoiler=Commands and Mentions][img]https://github.com/xadamxk/HF-Convo-Anywhere/blob/main/store/6-commands-and-mentions.png?raw=true[/img][/spoiler]
[*][size=large]Never Lose Your Place[/size]
A "New messages" line marks what came in while you were away, with a button to jump to the newest.
[spoiler=New Messages][img]https://github.com/xadamxk/HF-Convo-Anywhere/blob/main/store/2-new-messages.png?raw=true[/img][/spoiler]
[*][size=large]Emoji and Images[/size]
An emoji picker with your recent emoji, plus images by attaching, pasting or dragging them in. Click any image to view it full size.
[spoiler=Emoji Picker][img]https://github.com/xadamxk/HF-Convo-Anywhere/blob/main/store/3-emoji.png?raw=true[/img][/spoiler]
[*][size=large]15 Color Themes[/size]
Make the dock yours: Gray, HF Purple and HFX Blue, Midnight and High Contrast, or something bolder like Synthwave, Ocean, Sunset and Crimson. Pick one and open HF pages change right away.
[spoiler=Themes][img]https://github.com/xadamxk/HF-Convo-Anywhere/blob/main/store/7-themes.png?raw=true[/img][/spoiler]
[*][size=large]Your Settings, One Page[/size]
Bubbles or list, left or right side, your theme, how many conversations to show, sounds and more.
[spoiler=Settings][img]https://github.com/xadamxk/HF-Convo-Anywhere/blob/main/store/5-settings.png?raw=true[/img][/spoiler]
[*][size=large]And More[/size]
Convo buttons on profiles and posts open the chat right in the dock, unsent messages follow you between pages, Convo's message sound (mentions only or every message for group chats), and an option to hide Stanley's notices in the global chat.
[/list]

[size=x-large]Privacy[/size]
HF Convo Anywhere only talks to Hack Forums, using your existing login, the same way convo.php does. It collects nothing and sends nothing to me or anyone else. The full source is on [url=https://github.com/xadamxk/HF-Convo-Anywhere]GitHub[/url], along with the [url=https://github.com/xadamxk/HF-Convo-Anywhere/blob/main/PRIVACY.md]privacy policy[/url].

[size=x-large]FAQ[/size]
[b]Where are the settings?[/b] Right-click the extension's icon and choose Options.
[b]Does it work on convo.php?[/b] It hides itself there, since Convo is already open.
[b]Does it work on mobile?[/b] No, it's for desktop Chrome and Firefox.
