Chat in Convo from anywhere on Hack Forums!

HF Convo Anywhere brings Hack Forums' Convo to every page of the site. Your direct messages, group chats and the global chat live in a Messenger-style dock, so you can keep browsing threads and still reply without keeping a convo.php tab open.

It's lightweight and does one thing: once installed, your chats are on every page, with every option on one simple settings page.

Features:
+ Chat dock on every Hack Forums page (hidden on convo.php, where Convo is already open)
+ Direct messages, group chats (Comrades) and the global chat
+ Bubbles or a Messenger-style list, on the left or right side of the page
+ Online, away and offline status, unread badges and typing indicators
+ A "New messages" line and jump button, so you never lose your place
+ Convo commands with a picker (/flip, /jackpot, /stats, /price and more)
+ @mentions with a member list, and replies to individual messages
+ Emoji picker with your recent emoji
+ Send images by attaching, pasting or dragging them in
+ Convo's message sound, with mentions-only or every-message options for group chats
+ Convo buttons on profiles and posts open the chat in the dock (Ctrl-click still opens convo.php)
+ Unsent messages are kept as you move between pages
+ Hide Stanley's automated notices in the global chat
+ Follows your convo.php settings: muted conversations stay silent, and /embed hide is respected

Every option is on the extension's settings page (right-click the toolbar icon > Options).

Privacy: HF Convo Anywhere only talks to Hack Forums, using your existing login. It collects nothing and sends nothing to the developer or anyone else.
